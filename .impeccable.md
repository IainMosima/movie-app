## Design Context

### Users
One person at home, picking up a show on a Mac, a phone, or a Hisense VIDAA TV (remote with a pointer, D-pad). The job is "resume in one press" and "get this episode into VLC".

### Brand Personality
Cinematic, calm, confident. The app gets out of the way of the film.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark (it is used in the evening, on a TV). Existing system: `bg-black`, zinc neutrals, purple-600 accent used sparingly — play affordances and progress only. Readable show names, never raw torrent release names.

### Design Principles
1. One obvious action per surface.
2. Readable names (`IT Welcome To Derry · S01E04`), never release names.
3. Big targets with a visible focus ring — it must work from a TV remote.
4. No card-in-card, no side-stripe borders, no gradient text.
5. Text carries hierarchy, not icons.
